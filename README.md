A two-sentence horror story in exactly 150 characters: a father locks his daughter's bedroom door every night to keep her safe — until she whispers through the keyhole that he locks her in *with it*. A Node program writes the story to `entry.txt`, guarded to exactly two sentences and at most 400 characters.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
